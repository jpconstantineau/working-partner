## GTD Natural Planning Model

Method: David Allen's Getting Things Done (GTD), Natural Planning Model.

Working Partner provides an independent synthesis and agent adaptation, not official GTD training or endorsement. The procedure, coaching language, checkpoint guidance, and examples are project-authored; paid manuals are not reproduced.

The five independent steps are purpose/principles, outcome vision, brainstorming, organizing, and next actions. Each skill includes its own REFERENCE.md with sources and evidence limits. Human materials are linked from the [learning index](docs/index.html).
