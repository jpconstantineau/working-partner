# Working Partner maintenance

- Before editing skills or documentation, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the naming, routing, audience, and attribution conventions.
- During review, apply [CODING_STANDARDS.md](CODING_STANDARDS.md).
- Before publishing changes, run `python3 scripts/check.py` and `npx skills@latest add . --list`.
- Keep repository-maintenance skills outside `skills/` and out of installation/release catalogues.
