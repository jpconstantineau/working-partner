# Reference: Natural Planning: brainstorming

## Purpose and fit

Generate project-relevant ideas before constraining them into a plan. Start with the project's purpose/outcome and current knowledge. The working output is an idea collection, not commitments.

David Allen's Getting Things Done (GTD) Natural Planning Model distinguishes purpose and principles, outcome vision, brainstorming, organizing, and next actions. This skill covers brainstorming only. Do not force a full planning exercise for every project, or confuse outcome alignment with choosing an implementation.

## Researched operations

1. Capture possibilities without immediate evaluation.
2. Explore gaps and alternative contributions.
3. Pass the resulting material to organizing when useful.

Not every project needs new brainstorming. Lack of facts may need research instead. Do not mandate idea-volume quotas or confuse ideas with endorsed plans. Preserve provenance and status so ideas can be compared later. Organizing is a possible next move, not a required additional skill installation.
