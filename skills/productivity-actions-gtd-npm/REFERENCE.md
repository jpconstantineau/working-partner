# Reference: next actions

Identify currently feasible, visible actions and keep blocked future steps separate.

The Natural Planning Model includes purpose/principles, outcome vision, brainstorming, organizing and next actions. These are distinct entry points, not mandatory gates. Identifying a next action does not authorize execution, scheduling or contacting anyone. Multiple independent actions may be ready; dependent future steps stay in project support.

## Sources and evidence limits

- [Official Natural Planning episode description](https://gettingthingsdone.com/2024/11/the-natural-planning-model/): confirms all five stages; its public description is not a full procedural transcript. Audio and paid materials were not reviewed.
- [Wayne Pepper’s official worked example](https://gettingthingsdone.com/2010/01/how-gtd-can-help-with-applying-to-college/): illustrates purpose, principles, vision, selective brainstorming and movement into a plan. One example, not proof of general effectiveness.
- [David Allen’s project/action Q&A](https://gettingthingsdone.com/2010/02/managing-projects-tips-from-david-allen/): distinguishes currently actionable work from dependent future steps. Reader comments are not treated as the method.
