# Reference: organizing

Turn available project ideas into just enough structure to see useful next work.

The Natural Planning Model includes purpose/principles, outcome vision, brainstorming, organizing and next actions. These are distinct entry points, not mandatory gates. This is project planning, not the GTD inbox-organizing workflow. Avoid invented estimates, owners and deadlines; a group of ideas is not an approved plan.

## Sources and evidence limits

- [Official Natural Planning episode description](https://gettingthingsdone.com/2024/11/the-natural-planning-model/): confirms all five stages; its public description is not a full procedural transcript. Audio and paid materials were not reviewed.
- [Wayne Pepper’s official worked example](https://gettingthingsdone.com/2010/01/how-gtd-can-help-with-applying-to-college/): illustrates purpose, principles, vision, selective brainstorming and movement into a plan. One example, not proof of general effectiveness.

- [David Allen interview transcript](https://josephbushnell.com/david-allen-interview/): supports purpose, success-picture, idea-generation and organization distinctions. Primary interview hosted by a third party; audio not cross-checked and promotional claims excluded.
