# Review standards

- Agent instructions should change behavior: keep method-specific decisions and routing cues, not user-facing installation caveats or generic restatements of host rules.
- Keep catalogues at the appropriate level of the selection hierarchy and retain a useful direct-assistance path.
- Keep user documentation focused on the product and learning; place delivery status and untested behavior in the PR.
- Preserve reliable method sources and required legal notices without importing migration history into product content.
- Check the full changed routing path, human learning page, and selective-install references after renaming or moving a skill.
